Put your voice recordings here to clone your own voice for narration.

3-6 files, 8-15 seconds each, named clip_01.wav / clip_02.wav / etc.
(mp3/m4a/flac also work — agents/voice_clone.py converts them.)

See RECORDING_CHECKLIST in agents/voice_clone.py for how to record them well.
Then set XTTS_SPEAKER_WAV=agents/assets/voices/creator in .env.
